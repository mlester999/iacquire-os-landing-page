# Automate with Marky

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** AI automation & custom software development for growing businesses
- **What they do:** Builds AI automations, AI agents, and custom digital systems; offers ready-to-use systems ($499+), custom builds ($1,500+), and ongoing support ($300+/month)
- **Target audience:** Business owners and operations teams needing lead capture, appointment booking, CRM automation, or custom workflows
- **Page goal:** Present three service tiers (ready-to-use, custom, support), showcase portfolio work, and capture calendar bookings

### Ways to Work
- **Purpose:** Present three service tiers (ready-to-use, custom, ongoing support) with pricing, scope, and best-for messaging.
- **Layout:** 3-column uniform grid (bento: 4 cols, 4 cells at 1×1 each; visually equivalent). Each column is a card with number badge (01/02/03 in orange), H3 title, price ("From $499" / "From $1,500" / "From $300/month"), descriptive paragraph, 4-bullet list (benefits), "Best for" label + audience description. Footer link per card ("Explore Ready-to-Use Systems", "See Custom Build Options", "See Support Options"). Brown background, cards have subtle border or transparency.

### Ready-to-Use Systems
- **Purpose:** Showcase 10–12 pre-built system templates with category filtering and pricing.
- **Layout:** Filter buttons (All 12, Automation 5, AI Agents 4, Web & Funnels 3) above. Masonry grid: 3 columns, 12 cells equal-width varying-height. Each cell is an article card with tag (e.g., "Automation"), H3 title, description paragraph, 4-bullet list of features, definition list (Typical range / Typical delivery with values), "Get this system" link, and "Core system ready" badge. Cards are semi-transparent brown with beige text.

### Portfolio / Case Studies ×6
Converged grid: uniform 2-col layout. Each entry alternates image L→R. Left column (even entries): screenshot/dashboard bleeding off-left; right column (odd entries): screenshot/dashboard centered. Each screenshot is feature ratio 1.65:1 or 1.82:1. Paired text block: H3 title ("HomeVeal Bathroom Remodeling: AI Lead-to-Appointment System", etc.), gray description, orange pill tags ("BUILT" or "AI AUTOMATION • AI AGENTS"), "Read the case study →" link (orange), "Open gallery" secondary link. Background brown.

### Process / "From First Call to System That Runs"
- **Purpose:** Visualize 5-step workflow.
- **Layout:** 5-column uniform grid (5 cells). Each step is a card with orange number badge (01–05), H3 title, gray description paragraph, and context label ("READY-TO-USE" or "CUSTOM" with refinement note). Cards on brown background, bordered or transparent. Progression is visual via numbering.

### Support & Optimization
- **Purpose:** Explain ongoing monthly support (monitoring, fixes, tuning, recommended inclusions).
- **Layout:** H2 section header, intro paragraph, card with "TYPICAL INCLUSIONS" label, 4-bullet list of services. Definition list below ("Recommended for live systems"). Secondary label "What support covers — and what it does not" links or expands further details. Brown background, beige text.

### Stack / Technologies
- **Purpose:** Display 6–8 integrations/platforms Mark uses (GoHighLevel, n8n, Vapi, AI Voice, AI Chatbot, Zapier, etc.)
- **Layout:** Horizontal flex row or pill badges listing tool names. Single-line stub; no description.

### Why This Approach (Philosophy)
- **Purpose:** 4 core principles.
- **Layout:** 4-column uniform grid (4 cells). Each cell: H3 principle ("Automation first, agents and code behind it", "Start with the outcome", "Ship, then sharpen", "Human in the loop", "Built to not break", "Yours to own" — or subset). Gray description. Brown background.

### About / Background
- **Purpose:** Introduce Mark and credentials.
- **Layout:** 2-col: left image (portrait or avatar, ~1.65:1 ratio), right text. H3 "Mark Lester Acak.", paragraph bio, definition-list-style credentials or experience callouts. Brown background, beige text.

### Final CTA
- **Purpose:** Restate main booking action.
- **Layout:** H2 "Ready to automate the next chapter?", "Schedule a Call" button (orange pill), "Connect" secondary link (beige text). Optional: small social/contact badges. Brown background.

### Footer
- **Purpose:** Navigation, contact links, legal.
- **Layout:** 3 columns: "SERVICES" (list of links: Automation, AI Agents, Web/SaaS/Shopify), "EXPLORE" (Systems, Projects, Support), "DIRECT" (Contact, Connect, Schedule a Call). Single-line copyright. Brown background, beige text, orange accents on primary links.

**Notable patterns:** Alternating case-study image placement (L→R→L→R→L→R). Color rhythm: orange accents (badges, buttons, tags, highlights) appear consistently across sections. Brown page background throughout. Beige body text; orange H3 titles in pricing tier. Rounded pill controls (buttons, badges). Grid types: 3-column masonry for systems, uniform for process steps, 2-column alternating for portfolio. No sticky sections except header. Focus-visible states honored; prefers-reduced-motion supported.
